HALLOWEEN EXPLORERS - GRADES 2 AND 3

Open index.html to choose from all 40 requested activities and 4 bonus sheets.
Choose Grade 2 or Grade 3. Every worksheet has fresh original content.
Use Type to fill answer boxes. Use Draw to solve mazes, circle pictures and sketch.
Use Undo for the last stroke, or Clear sheet to reset the current grade and activity.
Answers and drawings save locally in this browser. They are practice worksheets,
not part of the forward-only Pumpkin Mystery assessment.
Whole sheet / Top half / Bottom half keeps one worksheet on one screen.
Print this sheet prints the complete current worksheet, including typed responses
and drawings, on one Letter page. Activities are available on screen.
The original Pumpkin Mystery remains the main start page.

All illustrations are original black-and-white vectors with white backgrounds.
Use Letter portrait when printing the current activity from the browser.
Word searches use across/down only. Grade 2 mazes use 10 x 10 cells; Grade 3
mazes use 14 x 14 cells. Each maze has exactly one route from start to finish.
The 4 bonuses are a second maze, word search, ghost dot-to-dot and monster design.

Open writing, odd-one-out and drawing tasks accept varied well-supported answers.
Canadian money tasks use coin values in cents, with no pennies.
The activity list describes the offered differentiation, not curriculum certification.

REUSE AND SOURCE
The story-map house and creature drawing approach is adapted into shared vector
operations. One content set generates both grade versions. This avoids
extra illustration generation and keeps print and screen prompts aligned.
The existing Pumpkin Mystery stories, scoring and student responses are unchanged.
worksheet-data.json contains all new content and teacher answer references.
